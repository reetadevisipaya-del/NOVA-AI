Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

NOVA is an AI-assisted planning application that helps users organize tasks, fixed commitments, focus time, and optional Google Calendar events. This Privacy Policy explains what information NOVA accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Information NOVA may collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, NOVA may process:

  • Account information such as your email address and profile details used to sign in to NOVA.
  • Planner information you enter, including tasks, projects, goals, deadlines, focus preferences, fixed events, and related notes.
  • Messages you send to the NOVA assistant and the planner context needed to generate planning responses.
  • Technical information needed to keep you signed in and operate the application, such as authentication session information.

2. Google Calendar data

Google Calendar access is optional. NOVA only requests Google Calendar permission after you choose to connect your Google account.

Data accessed

NOVA uses the Google Calendar events permission to read and synchronize calendar event information from your connected Google Calendar. This can include event titles, start and end times, descriptions, locations, all-day status, event identifiers, and related calendar metadata returned by the Google Calendar API.

How Google Calendar data is used

NOVA uses Google Calendar data only to provide user-facing planning features, including:

  • Showing your existing calendar commitments inside NOVA.
  • Preventing NOVA from scheduling focus work on top of fixed calendar events.
  • Allowing you to create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events from NOVA when you explicitly request those actions.
  • Keeping NOVA's local calendar view synchronized with your Google Calendar.
  • Using calendar availability as context for your personal daily plan.

NOVA does not sell Google user data, use Google Calendar data for advertising, or use Google Calendar data to build advertising profiles.

3. Google API Limited Use

NOVA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the prominent user-facing features described in this Privacy Policy. NOVA does not transfer Google user data to third parties for advertising, credit-worthiness, lending, or other unrelated purposes.

4. Storage and security

NOVA stores application data in its backend database. When you connect Google Calendar, OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored server-side and are not intentionally exposed in the public website code. Synced calendar event records may be stored in NOVA's database so the planner can display and plan around them.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect stored information. However, no internet service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

5. AI-assisted planning

When you use NOVA's AI assistant, NOVA may send the message you submit together with relevant planner context to the server-side AI service configured for NOVA so that it can generate a planning response. NOVA does not use Google Calendar data for advertising or unrelated model training purposes.

6. Sharing of information

NOVA does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate NOVA, such as authentication, database, hosting, Google Calendar API, and AI infrastructure providers. Data may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or with your explicit direction.

7. Data retention

NOVA may retain account, planner, and synchronized calendar data for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Google OAuth credentials and synchronized Google Calendar data are retained while the connection is needed for the feature unless access is revoked or deletion is requested.

8. Your choices and controls

  • You do not have to connect Google Calendar to use NOVA's non-calendar features.
  • You can revoke NOVA's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account's third-party access/security settings.
  • You can request deletion of stored Google Calendar connection data and other personal information by contacting us at the email below.
  • You can choose not to provide optional planner information.

9. Children's privacy

NOVA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and address the request.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when NOVA's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Material changes that affect how Google user data is used will be reflected here before the new use is introduced.
